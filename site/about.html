Capsule Toys

A capsule toy machine for your iPhone. Turn the knob, and a random 3D model opens in augmented reality: hats and glasses on your face, spacecraft on your floor.

There's no app and no 3D engine on the page. Safari's AR Quick Look does the 3D; the page only links to the model the right way, with rel="ar" and a picture inside: 

<a rel="ar" href="/models/crown.usdz">
  <img src="/thumbs/crown.png" alt="Royal crown">
</a>

Everything after a # sets up the banner inside AR: a title, a price, a button or Apple Pay, or a page of your own. Every toy's name and credit is a page like that: 

/models/crown.usdz#custom=https://ar.avolkov.dev/banner/crown.html

It works in Safari on iPhone and iPad, and on Apple Vision Pro. Hats and glasses need Face ID's front camera. On other devices, the machine shows a QR code to play on an iPhone.
