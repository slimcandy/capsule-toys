What AR Quick Look can do today

Use cases

The machine is a game. These are four made-up websites that use the same trick for real work, each showing a different part of it. Open them on an iPhone and tap a picture.

  • Harbour & Hill, estate agents A flat for sale No photos: the listing is an iPhone scan you put on your table. A button inside AR books a viewing. Button in AR
  • Perigee, a science magazine An article with 3D figures The Moon landing in your room, with captions in AR. The Mars rover stays life-size: no pinching it smaller. Captions · real size
  • Hollow Lantern, costumes A Halloween shop Masks and hats on your face, with the front camera. Apple Pay right there in AR. On your face · Apple Pay
  • Glint, opticians Glasses to try on Frames on your face, under a banner the optician designed in HTML instead of Apple's. Your own banner

This project started in 2023 as shops like these, mostly in Russian, with models I'd bought or couldn't trace the licence of. They're rebuilt here in English, with the models the machine uses.
