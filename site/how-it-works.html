How it works

There's no app and no 3D engine on this page. Safari on an iPhone already knows how to show a 3D model in your room: it's called AR Quick Look. A web page only has to link to the model the right way, with rel="ar" and a picture inside: 

<a rel="ar" href="/models/crown.usdz">
  <img src="/thumbs/crown.png" alt="Royal crown">
</a>

Everything after a # in the link sets up a banner at the bottom of the AR view: a title, a price, a button or Apple Pay, or a page of your own. A tap on the button closes AR and sends a message back to the page.

It works in Safari on iPhone and iPad, and on Apple Vision Pro. Hats, masks and glasses need Face ID's front camera.
